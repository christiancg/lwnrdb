The comparison of a field filter.

## Contains
The field contains the value.

## Equal
The field equals the value.

## GreaterThan
The field is greater than the value.

## GreaterThanOrEqual
The field is greater than or equal to the value.

## In
The field equals one of the values.

## NotEqual
The field is present and not equal to the value.

## NotIn
The field equals none of the values.

## SmallerThan
The field is smaller than the value.

## SmallerThanOrEqual
The field is smaller than or equal to the value.
