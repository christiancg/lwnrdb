The comparisons of a field filter.

## EQUALS

Equal.

## NOT_EQUALS

Not equal.

## GREATER_THAN

Greater.

## GREATER_THAN_EQUALS

Greater or equal.

## SMALLER_THAN

Smaller.

## SMALLER_THAN_EQUALS

Smaller or equal.

## IN

Equal to one of a list.

## NOT_IN

Equal to none of a list.

## CONTAINS

Holds a substring or an array element.
